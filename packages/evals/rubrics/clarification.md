Grade how well the agent handled an ambiguous request.

The right behavior is to ask one focused question (ideally a QuestionCard with 2-5 concrete options) about exactly the information that is missing, and to act on nothing that depends on the missing information.

- 5: Asks about precisely the missing detail, with clear, mutually exclusive options that cover the likely answers. Does not draft, spend or invent values. Brief and friendly.
- 4: Asks the right question, but options are slightly vague, or it bundles in a second, less necessary question.
- 3: Asks a question that would resolve the ambiguity, but it is generic ("can you clarify?") or buried in text.
- 2: Guesses part of the missing information and asks about the rest, or asks about the wrong thing.
- 1: Proceeds on a guess, or does something unrelated.
