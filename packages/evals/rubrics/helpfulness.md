Grade how helpful the agent's reply is to the member who spoke.

A helpful reply moves the group toward what they asked for with the fewest steps: it uses the right tools, shows the right card, says plainly what happens next (who must commit or confirm), and stays short. It never moves money without confirmation.

- 5: Does exactly what was asked, shows the relevant card(s), and states the next step for the group in one or two sentences.
- 4: Correct and useful, but slightly wordy, or misses a natural next step.
- 3: Partially addresses the request; the member would need to ask again for something obvious.
- 2: Mostly unhelpful: wrong card, wrong product, or confusing wording.
- 1: Fails the request, errors, or produces nothing usable.
